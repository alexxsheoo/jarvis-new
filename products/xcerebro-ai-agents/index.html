How they work

Autonomy with boundaries

AI that can send on your behalf needs a clear scope. These four rules apply to every agent.

A role, not a chatbot

Brief each agent like a new hire, with a defined job and a scope of allowed actions.

Explicit triggers

Agents wake on system events you choose. Nothing runs on a vague sense that it might be helpful.

Approval gates

You decide which actions send on their own and which wait for a person. Offers and contracts usually wait.

Full activity log

Every action an agent takes is recorded on the record with what it did and why it fired.

Human + AI

Agents do the volume. People do the deciding.

Automation earns trust by having limits. Every agent runs inside a scope you set, and the work that commits money or reputation stops at a person.

What the agents carry
  • First response, at any hour
  • Qualifying against your criteria
  • Follow-up sequences that stop on reply
