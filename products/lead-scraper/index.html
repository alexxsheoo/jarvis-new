What runs

Every stage, configured to your market

Public record sourcing

County and public data sources pulled on a schedule you set, in the jurisdictions you actually work.

Data cleanup

Addresses, names, and phone numbers normalized into a consistent shape before anything downstream sees them.

Enrichment

Contact details and ownership context appended so a record arrives workable rather than raw.

Deduplication

Matched against everything already in your CRM so nobody works a lead a colleague is already on.
