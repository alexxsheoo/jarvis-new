Jarvis CRM / features

Everything the revenue workflow needs, in one place

The point of a CRM is that nothing lives outside it. These are the parts of the day-to-day that stop being separate tools once the work runs here.

CRM & contacts

One record per person and company, with every call, message, note, and file attached to it. No parallel spreadsheet, no second source of truth.

Custom pipelines

Build the stages your business actually uses, as many pipelines as you need. Acquisitions, listings, and service jobs do not have to share one board.

SMS, email & calling

Two-way conversations in one thread, whichever channel the lead used. Replies land on the record instead of in someone's personal inbox.

Workflow automation

Trigger the follow-up, the task, the reminder, and the stage change from what happens in the pipeline, so the process runs without anyone remembering it.

Calendars & appointments

Shared availability, booking links, reminders, and reschedules, written straight onto the deal rather than living in a separate scheduler.

Forms & funnels

Capture pages and forms that create the record on submit, already assigned and already in the right pipeline stage.

Reporting & dashboards

Pipeline value, stage movement, response times, and activity by owner, reported from the same records the team works in.

Every feature above is part of the Jarvis CRM subscription.
