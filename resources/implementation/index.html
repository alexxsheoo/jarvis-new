Implementation guide

What a Jarvis build looks like from first conversation to going live.

  1. 01

    Map

    We document how your business actually sells, including stages, roles, handoffs, and the rules you already run by.

  2. 02

    Build

    Pipelines, workflows, and AI staff are configured to that map. Not a template with your logo on it.

  3. 03

    Connect

    Phone, email, calendar, ads, payments, and data sources are wired into one record per contact.

  4. 04

    Run

    You go live with your team, with approval gates where you want them and reporting on what moved.

Most builds go live in two to six weeks. The variable is how many integrations and custom workflows are in scope. Mapping happens first so that number is known before anything is built.
